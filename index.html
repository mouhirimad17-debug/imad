HOW TO PLAY

  • Fly — drag anywhere on screen (or mouse / WASD on desktop).
  • Fire — automatic. Just aim by moving.
  • FOCUS — hold the ◈ button to slow time, raise a deflector that destroys enemy bullets, and fire a concentrated beam. Drains the Focus meter — use it in clutch moments.
  • Shield regenerates when you avoid damage. Hull does not — grab Repair drops.
  • Clear a wave, then spend Salvage in the Field Workshop.
  • Survive 12 waves & 3 bosses, then go Endless.
